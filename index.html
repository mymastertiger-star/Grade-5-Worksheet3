قطعة القراءة (Sue's Diary)

Dear Diary,

Yesterday, I woke up early. I didn't want to stay at home, so I called my friend Katie and asked her to hang out together. I met her in town with her parents and went to the mall. First, we went to a computer store because I wanted to buy a new computer game. I didn't like any of the games, so I didn't get one. Then, Katie and I went to the sporting goods store. Katie wanted to buy a backpack for her camping trip. She found a great green one. It was really big!

We were walking by Green Park when we saw a nice coffee shop. I sat there with her parents to have some ice cream. I got chocolate, but Katie ate strawberry because she doesn't like chocolate. The weather was beautiful and we wanted to take a picture to remember our fun day. I looked in my bag for my cell phone, but it wasn't there. I was crying about my lost cell phone when I suddenly saw my sister and our cousin, Henry. I told her that I lost my cell phone, and she called Mom and asked her to look for it at home. Mom looked on my desk and then in my school bag and finally under my bed. It wasn't anywhere! I was really sad because it was a present from my grandmother. Suddenly, my sister's phone rang! It was Mom! She said that when she walked into the kitchen she found my cell phone on the kitchen table! I was so happy!

الترجمة العربية: مذكراتي العزيزة، يوم أمس استيقظت مبكراً ولم أرد البقاء في المنزل، فاتصلت بصديقتي كيتي وشاهدنا بعضنا في السوق. ذهبنا لمحل الكمبيوتر ثم لمحل المستلزمات الرياضية واشترت كيتي حقيبة ظهر خضراء كبيرة. تناولنا المثلجات في مقهى عند الحديقة، ثم اكتشفت أن هاتفي مفقود! اتصلت أختي بوالدتي للبحث عنه في المنزل وبعد البحث تحت السرير وعلى المكتب، وجدته أخيراً على طاولة المطبخ!

التحقق من صحة العبارات (True / False):

  • 1. Sue stayed at home yesterday. ❌ False (She went out with Katie).
  • 2. Sue wanted a computer game. ❌ False (She didn't find any game she liked).
  • 3. Katie likes chocolate ice cream. ❌ False (Katie doesn't like chocolate, she ate strawberry).
  • 4. Sue's cousin called Sue's mom. ❌ False (Her sister called mom).
  • 5. Sue's cell phone was in the kitchen. ✔️ True (Mom found it on the kitchen table).
